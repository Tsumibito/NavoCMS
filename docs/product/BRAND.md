# Project identity

- **Name:** NavoCMS
- **Pronunciation:** “NAH-vo C-M-S”
- **Canonical spelling:** `NavoCMS` in prose; `navocms` in package, CLI, namespace, and domain names
- **Tagline:** “The agent-native CMS. No admin panel. Your agent is the interface.”
- **Website:** [navocms.com](https://navocms.com)
- **Source:** [github.com/Tsumibito/NavoCMS](https://github.com/Tsumibito/NavoCMS)
- **Contract namespace:** `navocms.io`

Do not describe the project as an autonomous website operator. NavoCMS gives agents bounded,
auditable capabilities and requires policy or human approval according to consequence.

The project does not yet publish a trademark policy. Until one exists, contributors should avoid
implying that forks, integrations, or hosted offerings are endorsed by the NavoCMS maintainers.
