# Code of Conduct

NavoCMS contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and different levels of experience.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and security concerns responsibly.
- Accept maintainer moderation intended to protect the community and project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption,
and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation, contact a maintainer through the least public appropriate GitHub channel.
For sensitive or security-related conduct, use the private reporting path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban participants based on severity and pattern of behavior.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing NavoCMS publicly.
